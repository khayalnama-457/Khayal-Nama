THE BEAUTY OF WORDS

Poetry & Shayari

Selected couplets, ghazals, and verses from timeless masters—complete with Hindi transliteration, English translation, and literary explanation (Tashreeh).

Mirza Ghalib GHAZAL
ہزاروں خواہشیں ایسی کہ ہر خواہش پہ دم نکلے
بہت نکلے مرے ارمان لیکن پھر بھی کم نکلے

हज़ारों ख़्वाहिशें ऐसी कि हर ख़्वाहिश पे दम निकले
बहुत निकले मिरे अरमान लेकिन फिर भी कम निकले

"Thousands of desires, each worth dying for; many of them I have realized, yet I yearn for more."

TASHREEH / MEANING

Ghalib captures the endless nature of human desire. Even after fulfilling countless wishes in life, the human heart remains restless and hungry for more.

Allama Iqbal PHILOSOPHY
خودی کو کر بلند اتنا کہ ہر تقدیر سے پہلے
خدا بندے سے خود پوچھے بتا تیری رضا کیا ہے

ख़ुदी को कर बुलंद इतना कि हर तक़दीर से पहले
ख़ुदा बंदे से ख़ुद पूछे बता तेरी रज़ा क्या है

"Elevate your selfhood so high that before every decree of destiny, God Himself asks His servant: Tell me, what is your will?"

TASHREEH / MEANING

Iqbal's core philosophy of 'Khudi' (self-realization and inner strength): when a person recognizes their true potential and character, they become the architect of their own fate.

Faiz Ahmed Faiz GHAZAL
گلوں میں رنگ بھرے بادِ نوبہار چلے
چلے بھی آؤ کہ گلشن کا کاروبار چلے

गुलों में रंग भरे बाद-ए-नौबहार चले
चले भी आओ कि गुलशन का कारोबार चले

"Let the fresh breeze of spring fill the flowers with color; come, so that the life of the garden may go on."

TASHREEH / MEANING

Written in prison, Faiz blends romantic longing with hope for freedom—calling upon the beloved (and the dawn of liberty) to bring life back to a silent world.

Mir Taqi Mir GHAZAL
پتّا پتّا بوٹا بوٹا حال ہمارا جانے ہے
جانے نہ جانے گل ہی نہ جانے باغ تو سارا جانے ہے

पत्ता पत्ता बूटा बूटा हाल हमारा जाने है
जाने न जाने गुल ही न जाने बाग़ तो सारा जाने है

"Every leaf and every branch knows my condition; whether the flower knows it or not, the entire garden knows."

TASHREEH / MEANING

A masterpiece of simplicity and pathos by Mir: the whole world recognizes the lover's devotion and pain, except the very beloved for whom it exists.

Jaun Elia MODERN VERSE
کتنی دلکش ہو تم کتنا دل جُو ہوں میں
کیا ستم ہے کہ ہم لوگ مر جائیں گے

कितनी दिलकश हो तुम कितना दिल-जू हूँ मैं
क्या सितम है कि हम लोग मर जाएँगे

"How captivating you are, and how deeply affectionate I am; what a tragedy it is that we are all destined to pass away."

TASHREEH / MEANING

Jaun reflects on human mortality—mourning the bittersweet truth that beauty, love, and sensitivity must eventually surrender to time.

Parveen Shakir GHAZAL
وہ تو خوشبو ہے ہواؤں میں بکھر جائے گا
مسئلہ پھول کا ہے پھول کدھر جائے گا

वो तो ख़ुशबू है हवाओं में बिखर जाएगा
मसअला फूल का है फूल किधर जाएगा

"He is like fragrance, he will scatter into the breeze; the real question is about the flower—where will the flower go?"

TASHREEH / MEANING

Using delicate imagery of flower and fragrance, Parveen Shakir portrays how one person can move on freely like wind, while the one who loved deeply remains rooted in memory.
