A MOMENT OF REFLECTION

Aqwal & Wisdom

Profound sayings, timeless quotes, and reflections on life, consciousness, and human character by legendary thinkers and sages.

Sufi Wisdom THOUGHT
انسان کی سوچ ہی اس کی اصل پہچان ہے

इंसान की सोच ही उसकी असली पहचान है।

"A person's thinking is their true identity."

REFLECTION

What a person thinks in solitude shapes their character, actions, and destiny in the open world. Inner mindset dictates external reality.

Hazrat Maulana Rumi LIFE & TRUTH
خاموشی خدا کی زبان ہے، باقی سب ترجمہ ہے

ख़ामोशी ईश्वर की भाषा है, बाक़ी सब अनुवाद है।

"Silence is the language of God, everything else is a poor translation."

REFLECTION

Deepest truths and spiritual realizations transcend human vocabulary. True wisdom often dwells in quiet contemplation rather than endless debate.

Allama Iqbal SUFI WISDOM
اپنے من میں ڈوب کر پا جا سراغِ زندگی
تو اگر میرا نہیں بنتا نہ بن، اپنا تو بن

अपने मन में डूब कर पा जा सुराग़-ए-ज़िंदगी
तू अगर मेरा नहीं बनता न बन, अपना तो बन

"Dive deep within your own soul to find the secret of life. If you cannot be mine, so be it—at least be true to yourself."

REFLECTION

External validation is fleeting. Self-awareness and authenticity before one's own conscience are the true prerequisites of a meaningful life.

Hazrat Ali (R.A.) LIFE & TRUTH
علم مال سے بہتر ہے کیونکہ علم تیری حفاظت کرتا ہے اور تجھے مال کی حفاظت کرنی پڑتی ہے

ज्ञान धन से श्रेष्ठ है क्योंकि ज्ञान तुम्हारी रक्षा करता है और तुम्हें धन की रक्षा करनी पड़ती है।

"Knowledge is better than wealth; knowledge guards you, whereas you have to guard wealth."

REFLECTION

Material possessions require constant maintenance and fear of loss, while wisdom and education become an permanent part of one's character.

Mirza Ghalib THOUGHT
رنج سے خوگر ہوا انساں تو مٹ جاتا ہے رنج<مشکلیں مجھ پر پڑیں اتنی کہ آساں ہو گئیں

रंज से ख़ूगर हुआ इंसां तो मिट जाता है रंज<مشकिलें मुझ पर पड़ीं इतनी कि आसां हो गईं

"When man becomes accustomed to grief, grief vanishes; so many hardships fell upon me that they became easy."

REFLECTION

Resilience is forged through adversity. When life tests us repeatedly, our capacity to endure expands, turning mountains into stepping stones.

Baba Bulleh Shah SUFI WISDOM
علموں بس کریں او یار… اک الف تینو درکار

इल्मों बस करें ओ यार… इक अलिफ़ तैनू दरकार

"End your endless bookish debates, my friend… a single core truth of divine unity is all you need."

REFLECTION

Intellectual clutter can distract from direct spiritual experience. Simplicity of heart and purity of intention outweigh complex dogmas.
